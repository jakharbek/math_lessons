The derivative is the limit that the slope of the secant approaches as the second point moves toward the first: $f'(x_0) = \lim\limits_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x_0+h)-f(x_0)}{h}$.

Ask AI
TutorThe numerator is how much the function grew, the denominator is how far we moved along the axis. Their ratio is the average rate over a segment of length $h$. For $f(x) = x^2$ at $x_0 = 1$ it equals $2 + h$: with $h = 0.1$ we get $2.1$, with $h = 0.01$ we get $2.01$. The limit is exactly $2$.
